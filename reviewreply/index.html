How it works

  1. Install the extension in Chrome.
  2. Open your reviews on Google Maps, Search or your Business Profile.
  3. Select a review, right-click and choose Write a reply with ReviewReply.
  4. Pick the language and tone, check the reply, then copy or insert it and post.

What you get

Sounds like you

Pick a tone (warm, professional or short) and save your business name and sign-off once.

Regional languages

Replies in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu or Hinglish, in the language's own script.

Handles bad reviews

Calm, professional replies to negative reviews that don't make things worse.

You stay in control

Nothing is posted automatically. You always review before posting.

Questions

Why reply to reviews at all?

Customers read replies, and Google says responding to reviews shows you value customers. Most small businesses skip it because it takes time. This makes it a one-click job.

Does it post replies by itself?

No. It only suggests a reply. You read it and click post.

Do I need to share my Google password?

No. It works inside your own logged-in Chrome. It never asks for your password.

Is it ready?

Yes. Install it free from the Chrome Web Store and get 10 replies a month. Pro (unlimited replies) is coming next; founding pre-orders get month 2 free.
