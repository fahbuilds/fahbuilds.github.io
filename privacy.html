Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Fah Builds is run by Santhebennur Khazi Touseef (Touseef Ahamed SK), an individual based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers this website and our apps: ReviewReply, ListingWriter and TutorDesk.

What we collect

  • Early-access sign-ups: the name, email and any details you send us.
  • ReviewReply and ListingWriter: the text you ask the extension to work on (a review, or your product facts). This is sent to our server and to an AI provider only to generate the reply or listing. We don't keep it after the reply is returned, except short-lived logs used to fix errors.
  • TutorDesk: student names, phone numbers and fee records are stored only on your phone. We don't upload them.
  • Payments and pre-orders: handled by Razorpay (and later possibly Gumroad), which share your name, email and phone with us so we can deliver your order. We never see or store your card or bank details.
  • Basic usage: counts such as how many replies you used this month, so we can apply plan limits.

ReviewReply (Chrome extension) in detail

  • What leaves your browser: only when you click "Write reply" or "Try again": the review text you selected or pasted, the star rating, the language and tone you picked, and the business name and signature if you filled them in.
  • Where it goes: to our server on Cloudflare Workers, which sends it to Google's Gemini API to write the reply and returns the reply to you. We don't store the review or the reply. While we use Gemini's free tier, Google may use the text it receives to improve its products; we'll move to a paid tier, where Google doesn't do this, before offering paid plans.
  • Install ID: the extension creates a random ID (not linked to your name, email or Google account) so we can count your free replies. Our server keeps a count per install ID per month (deleted after about 40 days) and a count per internet address per day to stop abuse (deleted after 2 days).
  • Stored on your computer only: your business name, signature, language and tone choices, in Chrome's extension storage.
  • Page access: the extension reads only the text you select and right-click. It writes to a page only when you click "Insert into page", and then only into the reply box you last clicked. It doesn't read your browsing history, other tabs or your Google account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't ask for your Google, marketplace or other passwords.
  • We don't post anything on your behalf without you clicking post.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the apps: hosting (for example Cloudflare or GitHub), an AI model provider to generate text, and payment providers. They process data only to provide their service to us.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account and payment records as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and tax requirements. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing us.

Children

Our apps are not meant for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
